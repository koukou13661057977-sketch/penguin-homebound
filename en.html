How to play

  1. Move

    Use WASD, arrow keys or the touch arrows. Hold Space to slide on ice.

    In first person, use WASD to move and ← or → to turn. On touchscreens, drag the right side to look.

  2. Catch fish

    Swim close to golden fish to catch them. Press E or tap Eat to recover energy. Keep 4 for the chicks.

  3. Plastic and nets

    Follow the fish as plastic drifts in. Floats mark nets. If you get snagged, hold the direction shown to back out.

  4. Landing and rescue

    Wait for the guide before landing. Use the action button at orange camps and near stranded penguins.

  5. Snow and wind

    Oil makes you lose warmth. Check your temperature before the ridge and shelter behind rocks in gusts.

Ocean notes

Oil

Oil makes feathers less waterproof and less insulating.

Drifting plastic

Winds and currents carry plastic to remote shores.

Discarded net

Lost fishing gear can entangle seabirds and other animals.

Visitors ashore

Penguins travel between the sea and their nests. Visitors should leave the path clear.
